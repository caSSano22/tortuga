— SWISS REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

Swiss DLT Securities Compliance

Tortuga operates under the Swiss Federal Act on the Adaptation of Federal Law to Developments in Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT Act).

1. ISIN Securities Registration

Every real estate tokenized bond issued by Tortuga SPVs is assigned an International Securities Identification Number (ISIN). This enables seamless settlement across institutional bank custodians and decentralized Robinhood Chain protocols.

2. SPV Bankruptcy Protection

Each property is held in an isolated Special Purpose Vehicle. If Tortuga Estate Inc. experiences corporate restructuring, property assets remain 100% legally owned by SPV bondholders.
