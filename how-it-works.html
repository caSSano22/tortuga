How Tortuga Real Estate Tokenization Works

Bridging institutional real estate assets to Robinhood Chain through Swiss-regulated SPV structures and AI automated yield engines.

STAGE 01 • SPV LEGAL ACQUISITION

Swiss Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) Structuring

Every real estate asset acquired by Tortuga is isolated inside an individual Swiss Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV). The SPV holds 100% legal title to the deed, protecting investor capital under Swiss DLT securities regulation.

STAGE 02 • TORTUGAOS TELEMETRY & MANAGEMENT

AI Operating System & Telemetry Controls

TortugaOS integrates directly with tenant leasing APIs, energy management, and operational maintenance feeds. AI models optimize tenancy rates and reduce operational overhead by up to 34%.

STAGE 03 • ISIN BOND ISSUANCE ON ROBINHOOD CHAIN

Onchain Bond Minting & ISIN Registration

SPV equity is issued as fractional, yield-bearing ledger bonds certified with international ISIN numbers. Smart contracts on Robinhood Chain enforce compliance, KYC/AML transfer restrictions, and secondary market liquidity.

STAGE 04 • CONTINUOUS YIELD SETTLEMENT

Sub-Second Rental Yield Payouts

Tenant rent payments collected in fiat or stablecoins are automatically routed to the SPV smart contract. Holders receive monthly or continuous yield payouts in USDC / USD-R straight to their wallets.
